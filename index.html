01/ about

About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Dartmouth College. My research interests lie in natural language processing, with a focus on LLM-based multi-agent systems and diffusion language models.

My recent work spans decentralized task routing for self-organizing multi-agent systems, collaboration between large and small language models on agentic tasks, hallucination diagnosis, and long-form text generation.
